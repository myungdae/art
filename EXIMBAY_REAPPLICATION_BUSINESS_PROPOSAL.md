## 1. Executive Summary

**ESL PLUS is an educational technology platform specializing in English as a Second Language (ESL) education matching services.** We operate a B2B/B2C advertising and information access platform connecting educational institutions, professional educators, and international students.

**We are NOT a job placement agency or recruitment service.** Our business model centers on:
- 📢 **Educational advertising services** (schools/institutions advertising their positions)
- 📚 **Educational database access** (institutions accessing qualified educator profiles)  
- 👨‍🏫 **Tutor profile visibility services** (independent educators marketing their services)

## 3. Why We Are NOT a Recruitment/Job Placement Service

### Legal and Operational Distinctions:

| Recruitment Agency | ESL PLUS (Our Model) |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| ❌ Charges placement fees | ✅ Charges advertising fees |
| ❌ Conducts candidate screening | ✅ Provides self-service platform |
| ❌ Negotiates employment terms | ✅ Facilitates direct communication |
| ❌ Takes commission from hires | ✅ Pre-paid advertising packages |
| ❌ Guarantees placements | ✅ Provides exposure and access |
| ❌ Acts as employment intermediary | ✅ Acts as marketing platform |

**Our Platform Role:**
- We are a **marketplace** (like Craigslist for education)
- We are an **advertising platform** (like Google Ads for schools)
- We are a **SaaS information service** (like database subscriptions)

**We do NOT:**
- Screen candidates for clients
- Guarantee hiring outcomes  
- Charge success-based fees
- Act as employer of record
- Process visa/work permits
- Negotiate contracts between parties
